★ Profile of the Day ★

"He's just shipping," sources say. For 23 years.

Quiet maintainer of 1,134 npm staples now holds 842,530 stars across portfolio. Locals say "we use ky every day and didn't even know."

By Devprint Staff Reporter · 08 May 2026
Above: Sorhus, photographed in his Stockholm office. (DEVPRINT)

STOCKHOLM — On a quiet Friday morning here, while most of the JavaScript world was still arguing about which framework to use, Sindre Sorhus pushed his 1,134th public repository. He did not announce it on social media. He did not blog about it. He simply pushed.

The repository in question is, by some accounts, "another small utility." Sorhus declined to elaborate. By the end of the working day, two of his other projects had been added to the dependency trees of an estimated 380,000 active codebases. None of these projects, taken individually, are large.

He shrugs. The shrug, captured by our photographer, has been described in some circles as "the Sorhus Shrug" — a subtle gesture that has come to define an entire generation of micro-libraries.

The Stack: still JavaScript, still winning

Of the maintainer's 100 most active repositories, 56% remain JavaScript-first; TypeScript adoption sits at a steady 13%. A surprising 11% Swift footprint hints at a parallel macOS practice that has produced quick-look-plugins, beloved by Apple developers.
